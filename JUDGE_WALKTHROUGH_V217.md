# SPY Sentinel AI — Judge Walkthrough

## 0:00–0:20 — What it is

SPY Sentinel AI is not designed to trade as often as possible.

It is designed to observe the market, prove the quality of its evidence, refuse unsafe learning, measure what would have happened, and require independent validation before a learned Challenger can replace the Champion.

## 0:20–0:45 — Real market proof

This Judge Proof Bundle shows unattended SPY observations collected automatically from Alpaca.

It also shows independently timestamped SPY and option observations with the current contract, bid, ask, midpoint, spread, and named Alpaca data sources.

No paper or live order was submitted by this evidence system.

## 0:45–1:10 — Evidence against itself

This is one of the most important parts of SPY Sentinel.

The original V196 collector contained fresh SPY prices, but its option context came from an older audit snapshot.

The system audit caught that weakness.

We did not rewrite or delete the records.

They were preserved, quarantined from clean learning, and replaced prospectively with independently timestamped option quotes.

SPY Sentinel can therefore preserve evidence that hurts its own case.

## 1:10–1:35 — Paper reality

The system shows bid, ask, midpoint, and spread.

Its counterfactual returns are research estimates—not claims that those prices would have been executable fills.

Real fills may be worse because of spread, slippage, latency, and liquidity.

## 1:35–2:00 — Learning without pretending to trade

SPY Sentinel shadow-tracks what happened after an observation even though no trade occurred.

Those later outcomes become labeled experiences.

Clean labeled experiences may become Challenger training evidence.

## 2:00–2:25 — Promotion firewall

Even when the minimum Challenger evidence threshold is reached:

- Challenger execution remains locked.
- Self-promotion remains locked.
- Independent validation remains required.
- Champion replacement remains locked.

The learning path is:

Observe → Evaluate → Experience Log → Outcome Label → Challenger → Independent Validation → Champion.

Every arrow is a gate.

## 2:25–2:40 — Reproducibility

The project includes a credential-free verifier that recomputes the saved evidence counts, safety claims, governance state, and artifact hashes.

The judge does not have to trust the dashboard—they can verify the underlying evidence.

## Closing

SPY Sentinel’s differentiator is not simply that it can trade.

It can explain what it observed, expose weaknesses in its own evidence, learn from opportunities it never traded, and prevent unvalidated learning from changing production behavior.
